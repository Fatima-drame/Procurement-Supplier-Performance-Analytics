## Project Overview

This project focuses on using Power BI to evaluate supplier performance, procurement efficiency, compliance, quality and cost-saving opportunities.

## Dataset

The dataset contains procurement records covering purchase orders, suppliers, product categories, pricing, quantities, delivery performance, defective units and compliance status.

| **Attribute** | **Details** |
|---|---|
| **Key fields** | PO ID, Supplier, Item Category, Order Date, Delivery Date, Unit Price, Negotiated Price, Quantity, Delivery Delay, Defective Units, Compliance, Order Status |

## Data Preparation

The dataset was prepared before analysis to ensure consistent and reliable reporting.

Key steps included:

- Reviewed data types and field structure
- Checked missing values and inconsistencies
- Standardised categorical fields
- Prepared date fields for time-based analysis
- Cleaned supplier, pricing and procurement fields
- Prepared calculated fields for analysis

## Key Analysis Areas

**Supplier Performance**  
Compared suppliers across compliance, delivery delays and defective units.

**Procurement Spend**  
Analysed procurement spend across product categories.

**Delivery Performance**  
Compared average delivery delays across suppliers and examined procurement efficiency.

**Supplier Quality**  
Compared defective units across suppliers to identify differences in recorded quality performance.

**Compliance**  
Examined supplier compliance status and overall compliance performance.

**Cost Savings**  
Compared negotiated savings against procurement pricing to evaluate cost-saving opportunities.

**Order Performance**  
Analysed cancelled orders by product category and purchase order trends over time.



## Key Insights

- **640 purchase orders** generated **38.39M** in procurement spend across **713K units**.
- **MRO had the highest spend at 154K**, followed by Office Supplies at **151K** and Electronics at **143K**.
- **Epsilon Group recorded the highest defective-unit volume at 178K**, followed by Beta Supplies at **147K** and Alpha Inc at **140K**.
- **Electronics had the highest number of cancelled orders at 14**, while Office Supplies and Packaging recorded **11 each**.
- **Average delivery time was 11 days**, with supplier averages ranging from **10.6 to 11.4 days**.



## Business Recommendations

1. **Prioritise supplier reviews** where delivery delays or defective units are comparatively high.
2. **Monitor high-spend categories** to identify opportunities for procurement optimisation.
3. **Review negotiated pricing** to identify further opportunities for cost reduction.
4. **Investigate cancelled orders** by category to identify potential operational issues.
5. **Track delivery and quality performance** regularly to support supplier management.
6. **Monitor procurement trends** across periods to identify changes in spend and order volume.
